# Amazon Sales & Business Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand business performance, product sales, customer preferences, and order trends.

I built an interactive Excel dashboard using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, and Slicers to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard allows users to explore sales performance across different categories, products, customer locations, order statuses, and payment methods.

### Sales Analysis

The dashboard covers five major areas:

1. **Category-wise Sales:** Compares revenue generated by different product categories.
2. **Product-wise Performance:** Analyzes the number of units sold for each product.
3. **Sales by Customer Location:** Compares sales contributions across different cities.
4. **Order Status Breakdown:** Shows completed, cancelled, and pending orders.
5. **Payment Method Distribution:** Analyzes customer preferences across different payment methods.

### Interactive Features

- Category slicer to filter sales by product category.
- Month slicer to analyze monthly performance.
- City slicer to explore location-wise sales.
- Order Status slicer to filter order information.
- Payment Method slicer to analyze payment preferences.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following findings were observed:

- **Electronics** generated the highest category-wise sales, contributing 129,950.
- **Home Appliances** ranked second with total sales of 105,000.
- **Miami** recorded the highest city-wise sales at 31,700.
- **Smartwatch** had the highest sales volume, with 105 units sold.
- **PayPal** was the most frequently used payment method, with 60 orders.
- **88 orders** were completed, while 77 were cancelled and 85 remained pending.
- **Olivia Wilson** was the highest-spending customer, with total purchases of 36,170.
